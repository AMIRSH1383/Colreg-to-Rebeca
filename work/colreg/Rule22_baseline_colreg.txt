Rule 22 — Visibility of Lights

(a) The lights prescribed in these Rules shall have an intensity as specified in Section 8 Annex I to these Regulation so as to be visible at the following minimum ranges: In vessels of 50 metres or more in length: -a masthead light, 6 miles; -a sidelight, 3 miles; -a sternlight, 3 miles; -a towing light, 3 miles; -a white, red, green or yellow all-round light, 3 miles.
